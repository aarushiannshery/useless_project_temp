### Project Description

**What Genre Is Your Life?** is a chaotic and entertaining interactive quiz that analyzes your questionable life choices and determines which movie genre best represents your current existence.

From academic survival horror to Bollywood family drama, users answer absurdly relatable questions and receive a dramatic cinematic diagnosis of their life.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

People go through life without knowing whether they're living in a psychological thriller, a low-budget comedy, or a documentary about procrastination.

This shocking lack of cinematic self-awareness has gone unnoticed for far too long.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

We created an unnecessarily dramatic quiz that analyzes your responses to everyday chaos and scientifically* determines your life's current movie genre.

*Absolutely no science was involved.
